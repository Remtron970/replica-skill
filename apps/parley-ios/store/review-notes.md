# App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

Parley is a chat assistant that runs an open-source language model on the device
using WebGPU. No account or login is needed, so there is no demo account.

To test: open the app, type any question and tap send. The first message downloads
the selected model's weights (about 1 GB for the default "Qwen 2.5 1.5B"); a
progress bar shows the download. After that, answers are generated on the device
and the app works offline. Choose "SmolLM2 360M" in the model menu at the top for
the quickest test download (about 0.3 GB).

Model weights are data files (neural-network parameters) from the public MLC/Hugging
Face model repositories. User chats are stored only on the device and are never sent
to us or to any third party. The app contains no ads, tracking or in-app purchases.

Content: answers are generated by an AI model and may be inaccurate. The app shows
a notice to that effect under the message box.
