Parley Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Parley is an AI chat assistant that runs on your own device. We built it so that your conversations stay with you. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Parley has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives your messages, and we never see what you type or what the assistant answers.

Where your data is stored

Your chats, custom instructions and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Deleting a chat, using “Delete all chats” in Settings, or removing the app deletes them. We cannot recover them, because we never had them.

Downloads the app makes

To run the AI on your device, Parley downloads the open-source model you choose (from public hosts such as Hugging Face, GitHub and jsDelivr) the first time you use it. These are ordinary file downloads. Your messages and any personal information are not included in them. Like any website, those hosts can see your IP address when you download a file, under their own privacy policies.

If you use the optional Ollama engine in the web version, your messages are sent only to the Ollama address you enter, which is normally your own computer.

Children

Parley does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
