Remtron5000 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 (accounts and image/video generation added)

Remtron5000 is an AI chat assistant that runs on your own device. We built it so that your conversations stay with you. This page explains exactly what happens to your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Remtron5000 has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives your messages, and we never see what you type or what the assistant answers.

Accounts

You can use Remtron5000 as a guest or create an account. Accounts exist only on the device where you create them: your username, display name and avatar color are saved in the app's local storage, and your password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash. Accounts are never sent to us or anyone else, and they don't sync between devices. You can delete your account at any time from the Account page, which also deletes all of its chats.

Where your data is stored

Your chats, custom instructions and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. Deleting a chat, using “Delete all chats” in Settings, or removing the app deletes them. We cannot recover them, because we never had them.

Image and video generation (optional)

The Create tab is off until you add your own fal.ai API key. When you use it, your prompt, any photo you choose to edit, and your key are sent directly from your device to fal.ai, which runs the image or video model you picked (for example from OpenAI, Google, ByteDance or Kuaishou) and bills your fal.ai account. fal.ai and the model provider handle that data under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your prompts, photos, results or key. Your key and your gallery list are stored only on your device.

Downloads the app makes

To run the AI on your device, Remtron5000 downloads the open-source model you choose (from public hosts such as Hugging Face, GitHub and jsDelivr) the first time you use it. These are ordinary file downloads. Your messages and any personal information are not included in them. Like any website, those hosts can see your IP address when you download a file, under their own privacy policies.

If you use the optional Ollama engine in the web version, your messages are sent only to the Ollama address you enter, which is normally your own computer.

Children

Remtron5000 does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
